Margaret Atwood's *The Handmaid's Tale* follows Offred in Gilead, a theocratic state that has stripped women of their rights. This graphic novel adaptation is illustrated by Renée Nault.

Utah has removed the graphic novel from public schools statewide under its sensitive-materials law. [The Salt Lake Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/education/2026/11/18/utah-school-book-ban-list-reaches/) included this edition in its November 18, 2026 list of statewide removals. The listing is for the graphic adaptation, not automatically every edition of Atwood's novel.
