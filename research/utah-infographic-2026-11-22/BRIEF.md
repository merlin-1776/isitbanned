# Utah book restrictions: infographic research brief

Snapshot: November 22, 2026. Working research, not publication-ready.

## Scope and strongest supported panels

1. **45 titles on Utah's statewide school-removal list.** IsItBanned's structured transcription of the Utah State Board of Education spreadsheet marked November 17, 2026 contains 45 entries. Source: https://schools.utah.gov/curr/librarymedia and `src/data/utah-statewide-2026-11-17.json`. This is about access in Utah public schools, not a ban on purchase or universal availability. Check the live official source again before publication.
2. **36 of 45 have a state-spreadsheet publication year of 2000 or later; 9 have a year before 2000.** 26 have a listed year of 2010 or later. These are spreadsheet years, not necessarily original publication years; editions and graphic adaptations complicate a 'classics' comparison. Do not label these as original publication dates until checked.
3. **14 of the 45 statewide entries (31%) are attributed to two authors:** Sarah J. Maas (8) and Ellen Hopkins (6). The counting unit is titles, not individual authors or challenges. This is a concentration statistic, not evidence about the motives behind removals.

## Additional Utah records — NOT a statewide total

Our site has 101 unique book records with a Utah location: the 45 above and 56 other Utah-related records. These are site coverage, not an exhaustive count of books banned anywhere in Utah. They can include challenges, historical district records and varied outcomes. Of the 56 other records, 55 have no source URL in the Utah location entry, and 40 have no `datePublished` field in the book frontmatter. A source might exist elsewhere; the entries need case-by-case verification before claiming a ban, its outcome or date.

## Author gender panel — work outstanding

Do not infer gender from names or a binary assumption. Compile public self-descriptions or reliable biographical sources for all named authors; accommodate nonbinary authors, multiple authors and unknowns; state whether the unit is books or unique authors. The inventory has an explicit unverified field. No defensible men/women percentages have been established yet.

## Current versus classics — work outstanding

'Classic' is subjective. Prefer reproducible original-publication-year cohorts, e.g., pre-2000, 2000–2009, 2010–2019, 2020 onward, plus unknown. Verify first-publication years separately from edition dates and the state spreadsheet. Label a separate curated 'classics' set only with an explicit criterion.

## Working inventory

`utah-title-inventory.csv` lists all 101 site records, their statewide-list match, current location details, available dates and explicit verification gaps. The state and site files are source snapshots, not evidence that the local set is complete. The statewide source lists trigger districts; these are not added to a separate local total to avoid double-counting.

Verify official source freshness and transcription; independently source district outcomes; confirm author gender via public sources and original publication years; document deduplication and the cohort/time window; specify unknowns and denominator; have counts recomputed from the audited file.
